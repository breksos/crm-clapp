BREKSOS CRM · VISUAL DIRECTIONS

Three points of view on the same product

All three share one data model, the same nine pages, global search (⌘K), saved views, empty states and the live agent move on the board. They differ in how they tell you who did it. Every artboard below is a live, clickable build: open a file to drag cards, sort, filter, reassign and switch light and dark.

A · INSTRUMENT
One colour means “an agent did this”. Everything else is grey.
B · CAST
Every person has a colour, and their agent wears it too.
C · TWO DESKS
People and agents sit in separate lanes, and agents get their own desk.
AInstrument

Thesis: a monochrome instrument where the only colour on screen is agent activity, so your eye goes straight to what the machines did.

What's different
Stages are a grey ramp, so colour is kept for agents alone. People are round, agents are square and amber, with ↳ and their owner's initials. Sidebar and mono numerals throughout, dense grid-ruled tables, a log-style home page. Suits the rep who works next to a terminal.

BCast

Thesis: the team is the interface. Each person owns a colour and their agent inherits it, so you read whose work it is before you read what it was.

What's different
Humans are solid colour dots, their agents are dashed outlines in the same hue. “Nova · Priya's” reads as one family. Stage is shown by position (a 4-step meter), not colour. Horizontal top nav, a softer card-based layout, a timeline-led home page, and a person grid for team performance. Warmest and most social of the three.

CTwo Desks

Thesis: agents are coworkers with their own desk. Their work gets a separate lane and a persistent side panel, so what the machines did never blends into what people did.

What's different
Dark-first. An Agent desk on every page holds approvals, what each agent is doing, and the live log, and agent moves land there. Timelines split into a People lane and an Agents lane. Tasks split by assignee type. Stages carry a real chromatic ramp (indigo → cyan → amber → orange) because violet is reserved for agents. Icon rail nav.
