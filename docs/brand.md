# Breksos CRM — the brand

The rationale `docs/work-orders/m6-brand.md` asks for: what the mark is, why, and where
the palette came from. Everything here is regenerated by one command —

```sh
python3 scripts/render-brand.py
```

— which writes `assets/icon.svg`, `assets/icon.png`, `src-tauri/icons/icon.ico`,
`assets/banner.png`, and a `/tmp/banner-128.png` preview at the size the library actually
draws the strip. There is no hand-traced artwork in this repository.

## The mark

**Lucide `square-kanban`**, from `lucide-static` 1.42.0, used unmodified at Lucide's own
stroke-width of 2, in bone `#F2EFE6` on a `#123B33` tile.

The name was verified against the installed package rather than assumed — Lucide renames
glyphs between releases, and 1.42.0 ships six things matching *kanban*
(`kanban`, `kanban-square`, `kanban-square-dashed`, `square-kanban`,
`square-dashed-kanban`, `folder-kanban`). `square-kanban` is the one the work order named
and it is present, so no substitution was needed. It is **not** one of the Feather-derived
icons, so only Lucide's ISC licence applies; it is credited in
[`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

It says "pipeline" without saying "CRM": columns with cards of different lengths is the
one picture that means *stages, and things at different depths in them*. A letterform
would have said nothing the app name does not already say.

**Geometry.** Full-bleed tile, corner radius `0.225 × 1024` — Apple's ratio, because the
Dock is where the corner is actually seen, and the Clatch library re-rounds every tile to
`0.26 × side` in CSS so the shelf is uniform by the launcher's doing rather than ours. The
Lucide 24-unit box is mapped to `0.72 × 1024`, which puts the glyph's visible extent
(the 18-unit rect plus its stroke) at **60% of the tile** — the proportion a glyph-on-tile
mark wants, and enough that the three bars stay separable down to 24px.

**Fill measured: 100% × 100%** — a full-bleed tile, as `icons.md` §2 requires. Measured by
`render-brand.py` itself, which prints it on every run.

**Light and dark.** The tile is opaque, so the mark carries its own ground and is identical
on both. Checked by compositing over `#FFFFFF` and `#181B1A` (`--ground`, dark — updated by
M8) at 16/24/32/64/128 px. At 16px
the bars merge into a solid field — unavoidable for any three-bar glyph at that size, and
the reason the `.ico` carries all seven sizes rather than one scaled 256.

**The `.ico`** is re-derived from the same 1024 raster on every run, at
16/24/32/48/64/128/256, so the Windows resource and `assets/icon.png` cannot drift. The
resampler computes per-target-pixel source bounds rather than an integer factor, because
1024 divides evenly by neither 24 nor 48 and the naive version silently cropped the last
16 rows.

## The palette

Green because these are deals, but **dark and desaturated**, so it reads as an instrument
rather than as money. The neutrals are biased toward the accent rather than pure grey,
which is what stops the chrome looking like a UI kit somebody themed.

| | |
|---|---|
| Tile ground | `#123B33` |
| Glyph | `#F2EFE6` |
| Accent | `#2E8B72` |
| Won · Lost · Due | `#2E8B72` · `#A6503F` · `#C08A2E` |
| Paper · Ink | `#F6F8F6` · `#14201C` |

The semantic three are a **separate axis** from the accent. Won sharing a value with the
accent is deliberate — a won deal is the app working. Overdue is not, and is never drawn
in accent green anywhere: if it were, the one thing demanding attention would dissolve
into the furniture. `src/styles.css` keeps `--due` distinct from `--accent` in both themes
for the same reason.

### The chrome uses darker variants, on purpose

The table above is the **mark's** palette — the values in `assets/icon.png` and
`assets/banner.png`, where they sit on a `#123B33` tile and have contrast to spare (bone on
the tile is 10.75:1). `src/styles.css` does **not** use two of them. Text and small UI marks
in the light theme sit on white or on `#F4F7F5`, and at that size the brand values do not
carry:

| role | brand value | on `#FFFFFF` | chrome value | on `#FFFFFF` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| accent | `#2E8B72` | 4.16:1 — fails AA | `#1C6B57` | **6.38:1** |
| due | `#C08A2E` | 3.04:1 — fails AA | `#9A6415` | **4.99:1** |
| lost | `#A6503F` | 5.47:1 | `#A6503F` | 5.47:1 — unchanged |

WCAG AA for body text is 4.5:1. `lost` already cleared it and was left exactly as the brand
draws it; the other two were darkened until they did. On `--ground` (`#F4F7F5`) rather than
pure white the chrome values come to 5.92:1 and 4.63:1 — still passing, which is the margin
the darkening was chosen for.

The dark theme maps to lighter variants for the same reason and has more room. **M8 replaced
both** — the first dark accent was a full-saturation mint that vibrated against a
near-black ground; `--accent`/`--won` is now `#63B69E` (6.59:1 on `--surface`, AA) and
`--due` is `#D3A76A` (7.19:1, AAA). See [`m8-visual.md`](work-orders/m8-visual.md) for the
measurements on every dark token, not just these two.

**The accessible values win.** Where this document and `src/styles.css` disagree on accent or
due, the stylesheet is right and this table is describing the mark, not the chrome. A
"Negotiation" label nobody can read is not a brand.

`#123B33` belongs to the mark and **is not a chrome token**. The app is paper and ink with
the accent used sparingly; the tile ground appears only on the icon and the banner.

`src/styles.css` carries the semantic token set derived from this palette — light and
dark, every token defined in bare `:root` before any media query or `[data-theme]` block
redefines it, because the default "system" theme state stamps nothing on the root and a
colour defined only inside a media query does not apply there. No colour from the M0
placeholder palette survives.

## The banner

**The motif is what the app does.** Three stage columns right of centre, and one deal card
caught in the air between the first two — the moment this app exists for. The column it
left shows the empty slot as an outline; the column it is entering has its top row clear;
a tapering accent trail reads back toward where it came from. It is ringed in the accent,
which is exactly what the window does for 1.2s when a snapshot shows a card somewhere new.

**Every card carries an attribution disc**: bone for the person, accent for an agent. That
is the whole thesis — every activity in this app records whether a person or a named agent
wrote it — and it is the one thing on the strip no other CRM's banner could carry. The
discs are 8 canvas units in radius, which is **64px at 3440 and 16px where the shelf draws
it**: the largest single element on a card, deliberately, because the first two passes
drew them small and they vanished at 128px.

**Checked at 128px tall, not at full size.** That check is what drove the design: the first
pass had 22px cards with two field bars and 6-unit discs, and at the size the library
renders it, all of it collapsed into grey bars. The cards got taller, the discs got bigger,
and the card in flight got a lane cleared for it.

**Nothing thinner than 6px at 3440.** The design canvas is 860×128 — exactly a quarter of
the asset — so the floor is 1.5 canvas units. The thinnest ink drawn is 4 units (16px): the
motion trail, the empty-slot outline, and the accent ring. The smallest field bar is 5
units (20px); the count dots are 9 units (36px) across.

**The left 40% is clear ground**, verified rather than asserted: `render-brand.py` scans
the leftmost 1376 px for any pixel that is not `#123B33` and reports what it finds. The
motif starts at canvas x=372 and the trail's leftmost tip at x=378, both clear of the 344
boundary where the launcher's scrim ends and the white app name sits.

**The palette is sampled from the icon.** Every colour on the strip is one of the four
brand constants or a straight `mix()` of two of them, computed at render time — there is no
second palette that can drift. The board is the ground one step toward bone; the cards two
steps; the discs jump to full bone or full accent, which is the value structure that makes
them legible at 128px.

`assets/banner.png` is **3440×512 — exactly 215:32**, 44 KB against a 2 MiB ceiling.

## Sizes against the limits

| | shipped | limit |
|---|---|---|
| `assets/icon.png` | 1024×1024 RGBA, 21 KB | 1024×1024, ≤ 1 MiB |
| `assets/banner.png` | 3440×512 (215:32), 44 KB | ≥ 3440×512, ≤ 2 MiB |
| `src-tauri/icons/icon.ico` | 7 sizes, 8 KB | must exist, or `tauri-build` fails |

## Handoff — M5 packaging

The banner is on disk but **nothing ships it yet**, and that is deliberate: the manifest
and the packaging script are not this milestone's to edit. Two one-line changes belong to
M5, and they go together — declaring the banner without copying it fails
`clatch validate` on the depot:

1. `clatch.json` — add `"banner": "assets/banner.png"` beside `"icon"`.
2. `scripts/package.sh` — copy it into `pkg/assets/` the way the icon already is.

`photos` stays absent until after M3: screenshots need a window to screenshot.
