## 1. The person's view (about 45 s)

**Show:** the Pipeline. Four stages, totals in USD and EUR, one won and one lost.

**Click** `Orbit line monitoring`. The deal opens in the right-hand panel: the timeline, the next
step, the contact.

**Collapse the panel** (`⌘\`) and expand it again.

> "This is an ordinary sales pipeline. I can do everything here by hand. Nobody needs a terminal."

## 2. The agent works, and you watch every step (about 2½ min)

**Paste to the agent:**

> Using only `crm -h`, wait 10 seconds between commands, and do this:
> look at the board; open `halvorsen-fleet-tracking`; log a call on it: "Lars signed off on the
> 3-year term"; complete the next step `send-redlined-contract-to-lars`; move the deal to won;
> then add a contact "Ingrid Berg" at `halvorsen-logistics`, title "Procurement".
> Say one line after each step.

**Narrate as it goes. Each step lands on its own, about 10 seconds apart:**

| the agent runs | what the room sees |
|---|---|
| `board` | a line in **Agent activity**; the window goes to Pipeline |
| `show halvorsen-fleet-tracking` | the deal opens in the panel |
| `log call …` | the call appears in the deal's timeline, highlighted in the agent's colour |
| `done send-redlined-contract-to-lars` | the overdue next step is gone; the header's overdue count drops |
| `move … won` | the card moves to Won and the totals change |
| `add contact "Ingrid Berg" …` | the window goes to **Contacts**; the new row is there and opens |

> "Every line here is something the agent did, under its name, in its colour, as it happened.
> Nothing happens in a back room. That's what being built on Clatch gives you."

## 3. A refusal is visible too (about 30 s)

**Paste:** *"Move `nothing-here` to won."*

**Show:** a **red** line in Agent activity with the exact message the agent got.

> "When the agent gets something wrong, you see that as well, not just a success log."

## 4. Your own pace (about 45 s)

Turn **Follow agent off** and collapse the panel.

**Paste:** *"Log a note on `orbit-line-monitoring`: 'Phase one kickoff booked'."*

**Show:** the window stays where you are. The activity line still arrives, and the collapsed
panel shows a **dot**. Click the line and you're on the record.

> "You decide whether you follow the agent around or keep doing your own work. Either way,
> nothing it does is out of your sight."

## Close (about 15 s)

> "One app, two operators: the person in the window and the agent on the command line, sharing
> one state. Every step the agent takes is visible and attributed to it."

---

## Don't show

- **Teams, sign-in, Settings → Account.** There are no screens for them yet.
- **Reports** and **Team.** They're still placeholders.
- **Editing a next step's due date.** It can't be done yet.
- **Anything improvised.** If someone asks for a live request, say "great idea, after the session".

## If something goes wrong

| what | do |
|---|---|
| the agent stalls or goes off script | stop it; say "agents are agents"; go on to step 4 by hand: drag a card, log a note |
| the window doesn't follow | check the **Follow agent** toggle; click the newest activity line |
| `crm: app is not running` | `clatch run com.breksos.crm`; it's up in about 2 seconds |
| the data is a mess | `crm close`, copy the two files back from `~/Desktop/CRM-data-demo-final/`, `clatch run com.breksos.crm` |
